Features
Everything you need, nothing you don't
Designed specifically for KWUST industrial training requirements no bloat, no complexity.

Weekly logbook

Submit structured weekly entries with activity descriptions, file attachments, and progress notes. Never miss a week with reminder notifications.

Supervisor grading

Supervisors review and grade each submission with written feedback. Students receive instant notifications when entries are reviewed.

Direct messaging

Students and supervisors communicate directly within the platform. No need for external email, everything stays in one place.

Progress tracking

A live dashboard shows weeks logged, reviewed entries, pending items, and overall completion percentage at a glance.

Report upload

Upload your final industrial training report directly through the platform for supervisor and admin review.

Role-based access

Students, supervisors, and admins each see only what's relevant to their role. Secure, private, and tamper-proof.
